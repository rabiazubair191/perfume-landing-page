Fragrant — Perfume Landing Page

A minimalist, modern product landing page for Fragrant, a luxury perfume brand. Built with clean HTML and CSS, featuring soft cool-toned gradients, elegant typography, interactive hover effects, and structured product sections.

Features

Header & Navigation: Includes the FRAGRANT brand title and smooth navigation links (Home, Overview, Video, Reviews, Perfumes, Faqs, Newsletter).

Hero Section: Showcases the tagline "ESSENCE OF ELEGANCE" alongside a featured perfume bottle image and an Order Now call-to-action button.

Brand Quote & Overview: Highlights the fragrance philosophy followed by a 6-point product overview grid (Wireless Connectivity, Long Battery Life, Sleek Design, Superior Sound Quality, Noise Isolation, Comfortable Fit) centered around a lifestyle visual.

Promo Video Banner: Full-width perfume showcase banner with a centered play button overlay.

Customer Reviews: Testimonial section featuring customer feedback (Joshima Lin) with pagination indicators.

Product Showcase ("Check Our Perfumes"): Displays featured perfumes in a clean 3-column layout with pricing.

Service Highlights: Three-column value proposition bar (100% High Quality Product, Free Shipping Charge, Money Back Guarantee).

FAQs Section: Clean expandable-style question and answer layout covering product authenticity, longevity, and returns.

Instagram Gallery (#BOSS): Six-photo aesthetic gallery with subtle zoom-on-hover animations.

Newsletter & Footer: Soft gradient footer with an email subscription input, social media icons, and copyright details.

Technologies Used

HTML5 — Semantic page structure

CSS3 — Flexbox, custom typography (Jost & Montserrat), linear gradients, and hover transitions

Font Awesome — UI and social media icons
